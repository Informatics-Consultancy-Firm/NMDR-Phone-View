3 · Reporting Status Rule
Reported (1) — A facility is considered to have reported for a given month if the value on any of the 21 data elements above is greater than zero for that facility-month, in any age band or sex, from any of the three sources.
Not Reported (0) — If no non-zero value is found across all 21 data elements for that facility and month, the facility is marked as not reported.
Form cutover: old-form values after March 2026 and new-form values before April 2026 are ignored — a facility must not appear to report on a form that was not in use. Community (HF04) is unaffected.

HF04 community data entered at Level-6 CHW/HTR/ETR units is rolled up to the parent Level-5 MCHP before this check is applied. A single non-zero community record is therefore sufficient to mark the MCHP as reported for that month.

The same Level-6 units that get rolled up for facility reporting are also tracked individually. Only facilities with at least one CHW are listed — a PHU with no Level-6 children does not appear.

CHW not reporting = 0  —  none of them does.

HF04 only, and no cutover. HF04 is the single form a CHW keys into, so the April 2026 facility form transition does not apply to CHW status — the old/new form rule is a facility concern. The 8 old-form and 8 new-form facility elements are never read at Level 6.
Independent of the facility flag. The same value rolls up to mark the parent PHU as reporting, but is kept at Level 6 as well, so a facility can read as reporting while an individual CHW under it is silent.
Rate convention. The CHW reporting rate counts reporting CHWs only in the denominator, matching the active-facility rule used elsewhere — a post that never reported would otherwise depress the rate permanently.

5 · Outputs

An Excluded Facilities list (labs, Marie Stopes, unrecognised types — with the reason for each), a ⚠ Wrong Form report showing month-by-month form coverage and every PHU that reported on the wrong form for the period, a CHW Tracking tree covering every Level-6 CHW post under a PHU (HF04 only) with per-CHW month grids and reporting / never-reported lists, reporting heatmaps (all facilities · active facilities only), geographic choropleth maps at national / district / chiefdom level, a district map of inactive (never-reported) facility counts, facility-level tracking tree, summary aggregation tables, and downloadable Excel / CSV / R / Python exports. A facility is active if it reported at least once in the selected period, otherwise inactive; reporting rates use active facilities only in the denominator.

Methodology — NMCP Malaria EPI Reporting Status Tool

1 · Facility Registry

All Level-5 (PHU) organisation units are retrieved from sl.dhis2.org/hmis23 via /api/organisationUnits with ancestor fields, giving district (Level-2) and chiefdom (Level-4). Each facility is classified by the last word of its display name using a suffix-first rule (getHFType()), with misspellings folded in (“Climic” → Clinic) and nursing homes (“Home”) treated as Clinic. Only the five assessed types are retained:

Hospital Clinic CHC CHP MCHP

Level-6 organisation units (CHW / ETR posts sitting under a PHU) are fetched in parallel. A childToL5 map links each Level-6 UID to its Level-5 parent, so community data reported at Level 6 is attributed to the parent facility. Level-6 units never appear as facilities in their own right.

2 · Exclusions BEFORE ANY DATA IS FETCHED

A facility is dropped entirely — from every analysis, denominator and export — if it is:

  • A laboratory (name contains “Laboratory”). Labs are not health facilities, yet many are typed Hospital in the source (e.g. “Bo Regional Hospital Laboratory”). Left in, they sit in the Hospital denominator forever reporting nothing and drag the Hospital reporting rate down for no real reason.
  • A Marie Stopes facility — excluded by NMCP request (specialised SRH provider).
  • Of an unrecognised type — the type cannot be derived from the facility name.

The full list of excluded facilities, with the reason for each, is shown in the Excluded Facilities tab and downloadable as CSV or Excel.

3 · Reporting Variables — three sources

Twenty-one data elements across three DHIS2 datasets. dx is queried at data-element level (no category option combo), so analytics aggregates every disaggregation automatically:

  • Old form & community — 3 age groups (0–59m / 5–14y / 15+y; community 2–59m treated as 0–59m). OPD on the old form uses its own two groups (0–59m / 5+y).
  • New form (2025_HF01) — 22 category option combos per element: 11 age categories (0-7d, 08-28d, 1-5m, 06-11m, 12-59m, 05-09y, 10-14y, 15-19y, 20-44y, 45-59y, 60+y) each split Male and Female. All 22 are named individually with their UIDs in the combo register below.

Nothing is dropped. A value entered against any single age/sex cell — one female 20–44y RDT positive, say — is enough to mark that facility-month as reported.

Why no COC-level query here. dx is queried at data-element level, so analytics aggregates the disaggregation for us and the request stays at 21 UIDs instead of ~180 dx.COC pairs. The register lists the combos data-entry staff actually key in — the cells on the form. Combos DHIS2 calculates from others are not reporting evidence in their own right and are not listed. If this tool is ever extended to output case counts it must switch to explicit dx.COC pairs, since sums over calculated combos would double-count.

4 · Form Cutover

New facility-form data entry starts April 2026. The old form is counted only up to March 2026 (≤ 202603); the new form only from April 2026 (≥ 202604). A value sitting outside its own form’s window is ignored — it must not make a facility look like it reported on a form that was not in use, so a PHU that entered data only on the wrong form is correctly counted as Not Reported for that month. Every such case is recorded per facility-month in the ⚠ Wrong Form tab, with the data elements involved and the value ignored, downloadable as CSV or Excel. The two form windows never overlap, so a facility-month can never take values from both facility forms. Community (HF04) is a separate dataset and is unaffected.

5 · Fetching — how all the data is pulled

One /api/analytics call per year covers the whole country:

/api/analytics.json?dimension=dx:<21 data elements>&dimension=pe:<months in year>&dimension=ou:LEVEL-5;LEVEL-6&paging=false&ignoreLimit=true

Querying ou:LEVEL-5;LEVEL-6 returns every PHU and every CHW / ETR post in one pass, so no org-unit batching is needed. Analytics returns only non-empty cells, and aggregates over all category option combos because dx is at data-element level. Each Level-6 row is rolled up to its Level-5 parent via childToL5; a Level-6 row carrying a facility data element is ignored, since facility forms are never entered at Level 6. Every request goes through the Apps Script CORS proxy with exponential-backoff retry.

6 · Reporting Status Rule

Reported = 1  —  the facility-month carries a non-zero value on any of the 21 data elements above, in any age band or sex, from any of the three sources, after the cutover rule is applied.

Not Reported = 0  —  no non-zero value on any of them.

Each facility-month also records the source split: facStatus (old or new facility form) and comStatus (HF04 community, rolled up from Level 6). A single non-zero community record is sufficient to mark the parent PHU as reported.

7 · Aggregation

Facility-month records are aggregated to district-month, district-year, chiefdom-month, and chiefdom-year levels. The reporting rate at each level is Reported facility-months ÷ Total facility-months × 100. Facilities that never reported in the selected window are excluded from the active denominator (the second heatmap and the “active” rate). Geographic rates are computed from the chiefdom-month aggregates and mapped to GeoJSON boundaries.

8 · CHW Tracking (Level 6)

Every Level-6 org unit whose Level-5 parent survived the exclusions is registered as a CHW / ETR post, carrying its DHIS2 name (CHW01 upward within a facility) and its parent’s district, chiefdom, facility and type. Only facilities with at least one CHW appear — a PHU with no Level-6 children is absent from this tab entirely.

A CHW is reporting for a month if any of the 5 HF04 Community Based Services data elements carries a non-zero value at that Level-6 unit. HF04 is the only form a CHW keys into, so the facility form cutover does not apply and no old/new-form split is involved. This is the same value that gets rolled up into the parent PHU for facility reporting status — here it is kept at Level 6 as well, so a facility can be reported while an individual CHW under it is silent.

The tree runs district → facility → CHW, sorted naturally so CHW02 precedes CHW10. Each CHW row shows a tick or dot per month, months reported, its rate, and whether it ever reported in the selected year. The CHW reporting rate uses reporting CHWs only in the denominator, mirroring the active-facility convention elsewhere in the tool. Downloads: reporting CHWs, never-reported CHWs, all CHWs with status, and one row per facility with a CHW count and its CHW-month totals.

9 · Wrong-Form Reporting

Each ignored out-of-window value is recorded against its PHU and month in ST.cutViol, keyed ouId|period|source, so several data elements entered on the same wrong form in the same month collapse into one row listing all of them. The ⚠ Wrong Form tab shows the monthly split of facility-months reporting on the old form, the new form and community, then lists every violation with district, chiefdom, facility, type, month, form used, rule broken, the data elements involved and the total value ignored. Nothing in this report feeds any calculation — it exists so the PHUs can be followed up.
